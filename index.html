Dimension Definitions (from "Designing Your Life" by Burnett & Evans)

  • Resources: Do you have the raw material, training, money, contacts — whatever it takes to pull this idea off?
  • Likability: How do you feel about this emotionally? Is this something that really turns you on, or not?
  • Confidence: Your emotional intuition telling you how confident you are that if you did this, it would actually happen.
  • Coherence: Does the plan make sense? How consistent is each option with your Workview and Lifeview?

(T) means "it will come with time" — I don't have the resources now, but I'm confident that at some point in my life I will have more than enough.

Guidelines for Reviewing Odyssey Plans

For Reviewers / Listeners

  • Ask good questions — Focus on curiosity to learn more about the plans
  • No critique or unwanted advice — Judgment is "the number one enemy of creativity"
  • Provide specific affirmations — Highlight what resonates positively in each plan
  • Practice generative listening — Listen to truly understand, not to respond

Session Structure

  • 8 minutes per person — 4 min presenting + 4 min discussing
  • Safe atmosphere — Create a supportive "Life Design Team" environment
  • Collaborative exploration — The goal is exploration, not evaluation

Core Mindset

"You are not (and should not be) alone... Life Design, like all design, is a team sport."

The purpose is to help the presenter hear their own truths reflected back — sometimes we don't know what we truly want until we say it out loud.
