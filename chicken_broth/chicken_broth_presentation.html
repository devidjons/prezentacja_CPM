Chicken Broth Masterclass

Comforting, nourishing, and easy to prepare

Why Chicken Broth?

  • Rich in minerals and collagen
  • Versatile base for soups, sauces, and grains
  • Perfect for cold weather comfort

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (or 2 lbs chicken parts)
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 3 celery stalks, chopped
  • 1 large onion, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 2 bay leaves · 10 peppercorns
  • Fresh parsley & thyme sprigs
  • 12 cups cold water
  • Salt to taste

Prep Tips

  • Rinse chicken and remove giblets
  • Roughly chop vegetables—no need to peel
  • Tie herbs with kitchen twine for easy removal

Cooking Steps

  1. Place chicken, vegetables, and aromatics in a large stockpot.
  2. Cover with cold water and bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. Skim foam during the first 20 minutes.
  4. Simmer on low for 2.5–3 hours, partially covered.
  5. Remove chicken and strain broth through a fine-mesh sieve.

Finishing Touches

  • Cool quickly using an ice bath around the pot
  • Refrigerate and remove solidified fat for a lighter broth
  • Adjust seasoning with salt and fresh herbs before serving

Serving Ideas

  • Sip warm with a squeeze of lemon
  • Use as the base for chicken noodle soup
  • Cook grains like rice or quinoa for added flavor
  • Freeze in portions for quick weeknight meals
